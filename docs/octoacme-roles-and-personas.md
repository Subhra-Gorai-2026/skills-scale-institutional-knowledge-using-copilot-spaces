# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Interactions
- Collaborate daily with Product Managers, Project Managers, and QA Engineers
- Review requirements and provide technical feedback to UX Designers and Architects

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Interactions
- Align vision and scope with Project Managers, Developers, and Stakeholders
- Partner with UX Designers to ensure solutions meet user needs

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Interactions
- Coordinate with all roles for alignment (PMs, Developers, QA, etc.)
- Escalate blockers to Product Managers and Stakeholders

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
Designs and validates user experience solutions. Ensures deliverables are intuitive, accessible, and satisfy customer requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Develop wireframes, prototypes, and interaction flows
- Collaborate with Product Manager on feature definition
- Work with Developers to address implementation constraints

### Interactions
- Partners with Product Managers on requirements and priorities
- Provides annotated designs and feedback to Developers
- May work with Stakeholders and QA Engineers during user testing

### Goals
- Improve end-user satisfaction and adoption
- Ensure accessibility and discoverability
- Reduce rework by validating with users early

### Typical Communication
- Design workshops, feedback sessions, annotated mockups, user test reports

---

## QA Engineer

### Role Summary
Ensures product quality through manual and automated testing practices. Verifies that features meet acceptance criteria and work as intended.

### Responsibilities
- Create test plans and cases based on requirements and designs
- Run automated and manual tests for each release
- Identify, log, and validate fixes for defects
- Report on quality metrics before release

### Interactions
- Collaborates with Developers to debug and address defects
- Works with Project Managers to plan test schedules
- Reviews acceptance criteria with Product Managers and UX Designers

### Goals
- Improve product reliability and confidence in releases
- Reduce escaped defects and regressions
- Shorten test cycles via automation

### Typical Communication
- Test reports, issue tracker comments, release sign-off reviews

---

## Architect / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Guides technical design, enforces architectural standards, and ensures scalable, maintainable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on architecture, patterns, and tool selection
- Review and approve major design changes
- Help manage technical debt and code quality
- Mentor Developers and provide technical leadership

### Interactions
- Works with Developers to resolve technical challenges
- Advises Product and Project Managers on feasibility and effort
- Collaborates with QA Engineers on testability and coverage

### Goals
- Ensure system scalability, performance, and technical excellence
- Minimize rework from architectural issues
- Foster team learning and engineering best practices

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings, architectural decision records, mentoring sessions

---

## Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Represents a group or function with an interest in the project's success, providing requirements, feedback, and approvals. May include business sponsors, support, or customer reps.

### Responsibilities
- Provide input on requirements and business priorities
- Review deliverables and participate in feedback sessions
- Approve milestones and releases
- Raise concerns or blockers as they arise

### Interactions
- Receives regular status updates from Project and Product Managers
- May join key demos, workshops, and release reviews

### Goals
- Ensure project outcomes align with business or customer needs
- Increase buy-in, reduce surprises, and surface risks early

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder meetings, demos, status reports, email updates
